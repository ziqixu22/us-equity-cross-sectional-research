# Accounting Data Model

**Flow variables** measure activity across a period: revenue, net income, operating income, gross profit, and capex. A duration fact may be quarterly, year-to-date, or annual; it must be classified before TTM aggregation. **Stock variables** measure a balance at a point: assets, liabilities, equity, cash, and shares outstanding. They are never summed across quarters.

A systematic-equities researcher cares because treating a six-month YTD revenue figure as a second independent quarter doubles counts; summing balance-sheet assets across quarters is economically meaningless.
